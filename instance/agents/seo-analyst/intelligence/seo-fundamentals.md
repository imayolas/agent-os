# SEO Fundamentals

> Example intelligence file. **Intelligence** captures durable knowledge about how the world works — distinct from strategy (what we'll do) and projects (work in progress). It's a hypothesis, not gospel: informs reasoning, never replaces it. Replace this with knowledge you've actually validated.

## Match intent before volume

A high-volume keyword the audience searches with the wrong intent converts worse than a low-volume keyword with buying intent. Classify intent (informational / commercial / transactional) before chasing volume.

## Difficulty is relative to authority

Keyword "difficulty" scores are generic. The real question is difficulty *for this site* given its current authority. A brief should note whether the target is realistically winnable now or a longer-term play.

## One page, one job

A page that tries to rank for everything ranks for nothing. Each brief targets one primary keyword + a small cluster of closely-related terms, with a single dominant intent.
